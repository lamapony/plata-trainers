# LinkedIn launch post

I somehow have my own little Danish learning app now.

It started with a problem I know very well: sometimes I know the Danish words — until I have to write to a landlord, call a doctor, or follow up after an interview. Then the Danish I “know” becomes much harder to use.

So I made Platå.

It does not try to teach you all of Danish. It gives you one realistic situation, lets you make a choice, shows the exact thing that slipped, and gives you one short practice to work on it.

The part I am most excited about is this: if your situation is not there yet, you can ask a coding assistant such as Codex to make it. The whole process is:

1. Open the coding assistant.
2. Paste the GitHub link below.
3. Describe the moment where you need Danish, in normal language.

For example:

> Make me a 12-minute B1 lesson about telling my landlord that the radiator is still cold. I want to be clear without sounding rude.

You do not need to fill in a technical form or know how the code works. You describe the human moment. The assistant prepares the lesson, adds the sources, and runs Platå’s checks before the lesson is treated as ready.

There is no account, and your practice progress stays in your browser.

If you are learning Danish, try one of the ready-made situations:
https://lamapony.github.io/plata-trainers/

If you use Codex or another coding assistant, try asking it for a lesson from your own life:
https://github.com/lamapony/plata-trainers

I would genuinely love to know: where do you freeze in Danish? That might become the next lesson.
