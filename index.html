Need a different conversation?

Describe the moment. Turn it into a checked lesson.

Tell a coding assistant what is about to happen: who you need to speak to, what you want to achieve, and what you do not want to sound like. The open Platå repository supplies the lesson structure and quality checks.

No forms, JSON, or lesson-design vocabulary. Ordinary language is enough.

For example, say this

“Create a 12-minute B1 Danish lesson about telling my landlord that the radiator is still cold. I want to be clear without sounding rude.”

The assistant can infer sensible defaults, add relevant sources, run the checks, and prepare the lesson for review.

  1. 1
    Share the repositoryGive the GitHub link to a coding assistant such as Codex.
  2. 2
    Describe the real momentExplain the person, goal, and tone in your own words.
