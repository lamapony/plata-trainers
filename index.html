A lesson for your life

A difficult Danish moment can become your lesson.

Maybe you need to call the doctor, answer a landlord, follow up after an interview, or say no without sounding cold. Give the Platå repository to a coding assistant such as Codex and describe the moment in ordinary language. The assistant builds practice around the words, tone, and choices you actually need.

No forms. No JSON. No lesson-design expertise. You explain the human situation; the repository supplies the lesson pattern and quality checks.

What you can say

“Use the Platå repository to create a 12-minute B1 Danish lesson about telling my landlord that the radiator is still cold. I want to be clear without sounding rude.”

That is enough to begin. The assistant can infer sensible defaults and only asks when a missing choice would change the lesson.
