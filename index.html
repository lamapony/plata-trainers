How it works

Match → Gym → Coach.

The Platå difference: Most Danish practice throws volume at you and hopes something sticks. Platå watches the exact moment you stall under real pressure, fixes that one gap with a short drill, and surfaces the single best next action. You leave every session knowing what actually moved the needle.

1The Match — a narrative lesson puts you under social pressure; your choices change the scene outcome.
2The Gym — when a scene exposes a reflex gap, a prescribed drill targets that weakness in 5–10 minutes.
3The Coach — your dashboard Today card picks one next action from local progress, not a wall of options.
4Private by default — progress stays in this browser; export JSON anytime to back up or move devices.
