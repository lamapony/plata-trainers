About Platå

Targeted practice for the Danish plateau.

Platå is a static, local-first practice product: seven situation lessons expose a precise language or social miss, and five focused drills help repair it. Recommendations are deterministic; the browser does not call an AI model.

    Product boundaries

    What it does — and what it does not.

    It does

    • Practice real Danish situations at roughly A2–B2.
    • Connect a visible miss to a short, specific repair.
    • Keep progress private in the browser and make it exportable.

    It does not

    • Provide a complete linear A2 → B2 curriculum.
    • Create or publish lessons from the live website.
    • Offer accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or an AI tutor.
