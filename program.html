About Platå

Practice the Danish moments that are hardest in real life.

Platå is for the awkward gap between knowing Danish and using it under pressure. Start with a realistic situation, notice the exact thing that slipped, then practise that one thing.

    A focused tool

    What Platå is — and what it is not.

    It does

    • Let you rehearse real Danish situations at roughly A2–B2.
    • Show the exact language or social detail that caused trouble.
    • Keep your progress in your browser, where you can export it.

    It does not

    • Teach the whole language from the beginning.
    • Pretend to be a live AI tutor.
    • Ask you to create an account or send your progress to the cloud.
